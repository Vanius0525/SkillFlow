\subsection{Source-cache repair and attention-path experiments}
\label{app:mechanism}

Repair/path tests share fresh donor-correct, receiver-incorrect and
no-skill-incorrect items within each row of Table~\ref{tab:mechanism-contrasts}.
Qwen3-8B/MedCalc retains $114/124$ candidates from $14$ calculators
($116$ pass the first two criteria). All $372$ baseline generations match
across suites, and all $48$ receiver-state writebacks are token-exact.
Repair layers are $8/12/16/24$, path-cut layers $8/16/24$, with writes,
masks and contrasts in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:cache}, \eqref{eq:reading-cut}
and \eqref{eq:repair-effects}.

\paragraph{Three repair contrasts.}
On one fixed evaluation set under one protocol, let $a_X(L)$ denote
accuracy for condition $X$. Suppressing $L$ on the right, define
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{cache}}(L)&=a_{SC}-a_S,\\
\Gamma(L)&=(a_{SC}-a_S)-(a_C-a_R),\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{read}}(L)&=a_{SC}-a_{SC+\mathrm{cut}}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:repair-effects}
\end{equation}
These accuracy contrasts measure cache benefit, interaction and reading
dependence. Positive $\Gamma$ means the joint gain exceeds the sum of
individual gains. Multiply by $100$ for percentage points.

\paragraph{Qwen3-8B/MedCalc truncation sensitivity.}
All outcomes are retained. At $L16$, $S$, $SC$ and $SC+\mathrm{cut}$
truncate $4$, $4$ and $3$ of $114$ outputs, respectively. The $SC-S$ gain is $38.6$
points, $38.5$ on $96$ items with untruncated baselines, and at least
$35.1$ under adversarial scoring of truncated $S/SC$ answers.
At $L24$, $SC/(SC+\mathrm{cut})$ truncate $3/28$ outputs without an
additional measured accuracy loss.

At $L24$, reader/source-self cuts retain $29/114$ and $110/114$ correct
answers, a $71.1$-point gap. Counting all $21$ truncated reader-cut
outputs as correct gives $50/114$, still below $110/114$ for source-self
cuts, which have no truncations. Both contrasts survive this reassignment.

\paragraph{Redundant combined cut.}
Under causal attention and positionwise operations, earlier tokens cannot
read the skill. Persistent reading cuts prevent altered skill states from
reaching later tokens. Induction over layers and generated tokens then
makes the combined cut redundant. This requires blocking all later reading.

\subsection{Complete-source and receiving-history controls}
\label{app:state-completeness}

\paragraph{Population and protocol.}
The $580$ model/question pairs retain fresh baseline errors
(Table~\ref{tab:state-baselines}). Qwen3-8B/MedCalc reuses $124$ frozen
candidates from its $49$-condition study. The seven other pools use at
most $124$ IDs in original order. One DeepSeek/MedCalc item
fails input-length preflight, leaving $456$ new pairs. These pools precede
repair outcomes and differ from Table~\ref{tab:mechanism-contrasts}'s
screened sets. They are neither full-benchmark samples nor independent
questions across models. Wrong uses the original distractor. Neutral
length-matches a fixed unrelated document by repetition or truncation.
It may still influence answers. All conditions use the repair settings
and evaluator checks in Appendix~\ref{app:detail}. Layers $8/16/24$
are block indices.

\paragraph{Conditions and numerical identities.}
The $24$ conditions are four baselines (donor, Wrong, Neutral, no skill),
$SC/SC_{\mathrm{exact}}$ at $L8/L16/L24$ in both receivers, and
$C_{\mathrm{all}}$, $B$, Full and Replay in each receiver
(Section~\ref{sec:mechanism}). $SC_{\mathrm{exact}}$ rewrites every source
K/V tensor after $SC$. Elementwise checks find zero source discrepancies.
Table~\ref{tab:state-baselines} records the sequence identities discussed
in Section~\ref{sec:mechanism}. They concern skill-position KV, not all
residual states. Replay can answer differently: Qwen3-8B/TheoremQA scores
$62/63$ versus the donor's $61/63$.

\paragraph{Paired uncertainty.}
Table~\ref{tab:state-depth-uncertainty} uses $10{,}000$ whole-skill-group
bootstrap resamples with percentile intervals. The seed is $20261024$
for the seven new cells. Qwen3-8B/MedCalc reuses its audited analysis.
Intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons, with
few groups in some cells. Adversarial scoring of capped outputs leaves
Qwen3-8B/MedCalc's early-minus-late gap at $63.7/64.5$ points in Wrong/Neutral.

\paragraph{Additional Qwen3-8B/MedCalc content and layer controls.}
With all-layer source coverage, correct-skill KV gives $29.8/25.0\%$
accuracy in Wrong/Neutral, unrelated-skill KV gives $4.8/6.5\%$, and a
second predeclared neutral text's KV gives $1.6/4.0\%$. Eight-layer
writes at $0$--$7$, $8$--$15$, $16$--$23$ and $24$--$31$ give
$3.2/3.2/1.6/4.0\%$ in Wrong and $0.8/4.0/4.8/5.6\%$ in Neutral.
These limited recoveries cannot identify minimum coverage by comparison
with all $36$ layers because the write budgets differ.


\paragraph{Audit.}
Independent processes verify hashes, conditions, cache writes, token
identities and both scoring orders. Labels reproduce accuracies, cap counts
and intervals for $10{,}944$ new and $6076$ reused records. Gates are reused.
