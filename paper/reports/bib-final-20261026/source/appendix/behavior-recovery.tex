\subsection{A sufficient condition for behavioral recovery}
\label{app:behavior-recovery}

After prefix prefill, flatten caches as $\mathbf z_X=(\mathbf c_X,\mathbf b_X)$.
Here $\mathbf c_X$ contains source KV at layers $0,\ldots,L$, and
$\mathbf b_X$ contains higher-layer source KV and $\mathcal B_X$.
The cache write gives $\mathbf z_{SC}=(\mathbf c_D,\mathbf b_S)$,
preserving $\mathbf b_S$. For $\varepsilon_X=\|\mathbf z_X-\mathbf z_D\|_2$,
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\varepsilon_S^2
 &=\|\mathbf c_S-\mathbf c_D\|_2^2+\|\mathbf b_S-\mathbf b_D\|_2^2,\\
\varepsilon_{SC}^2&=\|\mathbf b_S-\mathbf b_D\|_2^2,\\
\varepsilon_S^2-\varepsilon_{SC}^2
 &=\|\mathbf c_S-\mathbf c_D\|_2^2\ge0.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:cache-distance}
\end{equation}
Here $\|\cdot\|_2$ is Euclidean length. Exact copying removes the replaced
entries' squared error from the cache distance, independently of causal
reconstruction.

\paragraph{Output stability.}
Fix the model, positions, last prompt token and mask. Let $F_t^D(\mathbf z)$
map initial caches to token-$t$ logits (unnormalized token scores) after
donor history $y_{D,<t}$ and its cache updates. For the compared states, assume
\begin{equation}
\|F_t^D(\mathbf z)-F_t^D(\mathbf z')\|_\infty
\le\kappa_t\|\mathbf z-\mathbf z'\|_2.
\label{eq:cache-lipschitz}
\end{equation}
The infinity norm bounds the largest absolute logit change. Let
$m_{D,t}>0$ be the donor's highest logit minus its second-highest.
Margin stability \citep{tsuzuku2018lipschitz} gives
\begin{equation}
[\ \forall t\le N:\ 2\kappa_t\varepsilon_{SC}<m_{D,t}\ ]
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \hat y_{SC}=\hat y_D,
\label{eq:sequence-recovery}
\end{equation}
where $N$ includes the stopping token and $\varepsilon_{SC}$ is defined
in Eq.~\eqref{eq:cache-distance}.
\emph{Proof.} Each logit changes by at most $\kappa_t\varepsilon_{SC}$,
so the donor's winning logit remains ahead by at least
$m_{D,t}-2\kappa_t\varepsilon_{SC}>0$. Induction over the shared greedy
history, including the common stopping rule, proves sequence equality.
\hfill$\square$

Different sequences may also answer correctly. A common sensitivity bound
gives $SC$ no larger error bound than $S$. Errors from different cache
components can offset one another, so replacing one component can remove
that offset and increase actual logit error. Accuracy need not improve. Sensitivities and
margins remain unverified. The guarantee excludes $SC+\mathrm{cut}$,
which changes the mask.
