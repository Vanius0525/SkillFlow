\subsection{Source-cache repair and attention-path experiments}
\label{app:mechanism}

Repair and path cuts use fresh donor-correct, receiver-incorrect and
no-skill-incorrect items, shared within each model/task row of
Table~\ref{tab:mechanism-contrasts}. For Qwen3-8B/MedCalc, this retains
$114$ of $124$ frozen candidates from $14$ calculators ($116$ pass the
first two criteria). All $372$ baseline generations agree token-for-token
between suites, and all $48$ receiver-state writebacks are token-exact.
Repair uses $L8/L12/L16/L24$; path cuts use $L8/L16/L24$.
Writes, masks and contrasts follow Eqs.~\eqref{eq:cache},
\eqref{eq:reading-cut} and \eqref{eq:repair-effects}.

\paragraph{Qwen3-8B/MedCalc truncation sensitivity.}
All outcomes are retained, including generation-limit truncations.
At $L16$, $S$, $SC$ and $SC+\mathrm{cut}$ truncate $4$, $4$ and $3$
of $114$ outputs.
The $SC-S$ gain is $38.6$ points, $38.5$ on the $96$ items with untruncated
baselines, and at least $35.1$ under adversarial scoring of truncated
$S/SC$ answers. At $L24$, $SC/(SC+\mathrm{cut})$ truncate $3/28$
outputs without an additional measured accuracy loss.

At layer $24$, reader and source-self cuts retain $29/114$ and $110/114$
correct answers. Their loss difference is $71.1$ points.
Reader cuts truncate $21$ outputs. Counting all as correct still leaves
retention at $50/114$, below source-self retention $110/114$ (no truncation).
Both contrasts survive worst-case reassignment.

\paragraph{Redundant combined cut.}
With causal attention and otherwise positionwise operations, earlier
tokens cannot read the skill. Persistent reading cuts also prevent its
changed states from reaching later tokens. Adding a within-skill cut
therefore leaves outputs unchanged by induction over layers and generated
tokens. We omit this redundant condition. The argument does not apply
when later reading is allowed.

\subsection{Complete-source and receiving-history controls}
\label{app:state-completeness}

\paragraph{Population and protocol.}
The eight cells use $580$ model--question pairs, retaining fresh baseline
errors (Table~\ref{tab:state-baselines}). Qwen3-8B/MedCalc reuses all $124$
frozen candidates from its $49$-condition study. The other seven cells use
pre-existing historical pools, capped at the first $124$ IDs in their original
order; one DeepSeek/MedCalc item fails input-length preflight, leaving $456$
new pairs. Selection precedes repair outcomes. These are selected pools,
not full-benchmark estimates or $580$ independent questions across models;
they differ from Table~\ref{tab:mechanism-contrasts}'s screened populations.
Wrong uses the original distractor. Neutral repeats or truncates one fixed
unrelated document to match source positions; it may still influence answers.
All conditions use BF16, eager attention, split prefill, greedy decoding and
$1536$ new tokens. TheoremQA resets parser globals per answer.
Layers $8/16/24$ are block indices, not matched fractions of model depth.

\paragraph{Conditions and numerical identities.}
The $24$ conditions comprise four baselines (donor, Wrong, Neutral,
no skill), $SC$ and $SC_{\mathrm{exact}}$ at $L8/L16/L24$ in both
receivers, and four further conditions per receiver: all-layer source-KV
copying, other-position KV copying, complete-prefix KV copying, and
all-layer source copying followed by replay of the later prompt.
$SC_{\mathrm{exact}}$ explicitly rewrites every source K/V tensor after
$SC$. Runtime elementwise checks find zero source discrepancies at all
layers in every measured $SC$ condition. The explicit rewrite preserves
all $3{,}480$ generated sequences across the eight cells. These identities
concern KV at skill positions, not all residual states or all prefix KV.
Other-position KV can still encode effects of earlier skill processing.

Complete-prefix copying reproduces all $1{,}160$ donor sequences.
Replay uses donor source KV and recomputes the later prompt; both require
same-question donor computation. Replay is not exact: Qwen3-8B/TheoremQA
scores $62/63$ versus the donor's $61/63$.

\paragraph{Paired uncertainty.}
Table~\ref{tab:state-depth-uncertainty} uses $10{,}000$ whole-skill-group
bootstrap resamples and percentile intervals (seed $20261024$ for the seven
new cells; Qwen3-8B/MedCalc reuses its audited analysis). Intervals are
exploratory, unadjusted for multiple comparisons, and some cells contain
few groups. Qwen3-8B/MedCalc's early-minus-late gap remains $63.7/64.5$
points under adversarial scoring of capped outputs in Wrong/Neutral.

\paragraph{Additional Qwen3-8B/MedCalc content and layer controls.}
With all-layer source coverage fixed, correct-skill KV gives
$29.8\%$ in Wrong and $25.0\%$ in Neutral. Unrelated-skill KV gives
$4.8/6.5\%$ and a second predeclared neutral text's KV gives
$1.6/4.0\%$, respectively. Four equally sized eight-layer source writes, at layers
$0$--$7$, $8$--$15$, $16$--$23$ and $24$--$31$, yield
$3.2/3.2/1.6/4.0\%$ in Wrong and $0.8/4.0/4.8/5.6\%$ in Neutral.
These windows individually recover little; comparison with all $36$
layers does not identify the minimum required coverage because the write
budgets differ.


\paragraph{Audit.}
Independent processes verify hashes, condition keys, cache writes, token
identities and official scores in both orders. Exported labels reproduce
all accuracies, cap counts and intervals for $10{,}944$ new and $6076$
reused formal records. Gate records are reused without regeneration.
