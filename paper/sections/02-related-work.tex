\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Transfer interventions and source dependence.}
Task and function vectors transfer behaviours from demonstrations
\citep{hendel2023taskvectors, todd2024functionvectors}. Natural-language
instructions can yield different function-vector components
\citep{davidson2025prompting}, and instruction-vector patches can interact
superadditively across layers \citep{bigoulaeva2026instructionvectors}.
Transfer also depends on time and semantic scope \citep{jit2025taskrepr}.
These results motivate testing continued source dependence separately:
\citet{sia2024incontexttranslation} use cumulative context masking, and
\citet{onset2025instruction} compare instruction-span and final-position
patches. \citet{yang2025internalcot} combine layer-from context masking with cross-task
patching to study subtask scheduling, reporting best-layer transfer strength.
Our target is the depth interval where source-span transfer has weakened but
cumulative skill-attention knockout still damages the original computation,
under matched task and model conditions.

\paragraph{What a transferred state measures.}
A multiple-choice answer symbol \citep{wiegreffe2025mcq} and states from
completed reasoning \citep{mehrafarin2026cothidden} differ from the skill
context supplied before generation. Sequential patching
\citep{dura2026sequentialpatching} and repeated activation steering
\citep{panickssery2024caa, liu2024icv, stolfo2025instruction} also intervene
beyond our one-time prefill protocol. Following activation-patching methodology
\citep{zhang2023bestpractices, heimersheim2024patching}, we use matched
controls, generated answers and identity checks to delimit what recovery
establishes. Our depth comparison extends beyond tracing factual recall
\citep{meng2022rome, geva2023dissecting} to the sustained use of skills.

\paragraph{Skills and compact context representations.}
Skill benchmarks evaluate retrieval and application
\citep{su2026srabench, skillsbench2026, sweskills2026}. Web-agent work supplies reusable workflows and skills to support browser
tasks \citep{wang2024awm, wang2026webxskill}. Such gains depend on evaluation
conditions: under a matched total token budget, online skill and memory
modules can lose their aggregate advantage \citep{hajimiri2026budgetskills}.
We hold skill selection fixed and study how injected content is used, rather
than proposing a skill-learning or retrieval system. Learned compression uses aggregated
activations \citep{ardoin2026promptcompression}, summary tokens
\citep{mu2023gist}, compact states \citep{ge2024icae} or weight updates
\citep{doc2lora2026, deepcontextdistill2025}. We intervene on naturally
occurring states without training a compressor.
