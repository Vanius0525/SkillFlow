## 3. Found on the way

### 3.1 Two 8B `e14` runs will not share a 4090

`tA-num-k61` OOMed beside `tA-num-k32` within six minutes. The captures are three
tensors of `36 layers x k positions x 4096` per item, held on the GPU for all 358
items: 19 GB at k=61, 10 GB at k=32, against 16 GB of weights each. One per box.

### 3.2 `d17-neutral`'s layers 24–26 were empty locally, and whole on the shared disk

The old mirror bug (`cp -ru` copying a file being written as 0 bytes and never
refreshing it) left three 0-row layer files in the local copy. One of the three
instance directories under `$B/wbout/` had all 28 layers complete. `fetch_ta.sh
--mirror` now takes the longest copy of each file, which is what `qrun.sh` does on
restore; all 28 layers are local and 358 rows each.

**This was not visible in any check**: `check_main_data.py` reads Table 1's tags, not
these, and `campaign_numbers.py` merged `d17-neutral-b` over the holes for the layers
it happened to cover (26, 27) — 24 and 25 were simply absent from the curve.

### 3.3 The appendix is still the 200-item pilot where the main text is the 358-item rerun

The same quantity is printed twice at two scales:

| display | scale | content component at L26 |
|---|---|---|
| §4.1, main text (line 310) | 358 items, rescued cell 82 | **0.805** |
| `tab:controls`, `tab:causal`, `tab:parperp`, `tab:ladder`, `fig:causal`, `fig:decomp` | 200 items, rescued cell 48 | **0.812** |

and the main text sends the reader to the appendix "for the full comparison". Every
arm those displays need (`add_d_par/perp`, `add_dbar`, `add_d_a0.5/a2`,
`add_d_samefam/crossfam`, the norms and cosines) exists in `d17-neutral` at 358 items
over 28 layers, so this needs no GPU time — only regeneration. `paper/decomp_tables.py`
(new) rebuilds the three tables, `paper/controls_table.py` (new) the controls table
once the two `-b` jobs land, and `paper/figs.py --e14 tierA=<dir>` the two figures.

Checked so far at 358 items: the par/perp crossover is still exactly layer 21
(0.098 shared vs 0.427 item-specific), `‖g‖ > ‖t‖` still holds on layers 0–16, and the
content component is 0.805 where the pilot said 0.812.

### 3.5 The two negative controls coincide in the quoted band, and only there

`d17-shuffled` and `d17-corrupted` both give 0.171–0.256 over layers 21–27 — the same
two endpoints. They are not the same curve: below 21 they differ by a factor of three
(L17: 0.098 vs 0.012; L20: 0.098 vs 0.049). The coincidence is in the band the paper
quotes, which is why the main text can say "0.17--0.26" for both at once. Worth a
sentence rather than silence, and the fill-in decides whether it survives layers 23
and 25.
